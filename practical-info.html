Please note

Travel, accommodation and insurance are not included in the congress fee.

All fees are stated in SEK excluding Swedish VAT. According to Swedish and EU legislation, Swedish VAT of 25% applies to the registration fee and social events.

Payment is made in Swedish krona (SEK). An invoice will be sent by email after registration.

Cancellation

Cancellations made before 1 April 2027 are eligible for a full refund, less an administration fee of SEK 500. After 1 April, registration is binding and no refunds will be made.

If you are unable to attend, your registration may be transferred to another colleague within your organisation. Changes and cancellations should be made in writing to info@congressoab.se.

TRAVEL

Getting to Uppsala

Uppsala is easy to reach from both Stockholm and Stockholm Arlanda Airport.

From Arlanda Airport

Direct trains to Uppsala depart from SkyCity between Terminals 4 and 5 and take approximately 18 minutes. Bus 801 is another option and takes around 50 minutes. Taxis are also available outside the terminals.

From Stockholm

Regional and commuter trains run frequently between Stockholm and Uppsala, with a journey time of approximately 30–50 minutes.

By car

Uppsala is located along the E4 motorway, approximately 70 km north of Stockholm.

For detailed travel information, timetables and links to transport operators, visit Destination Uppsala – Travelling to Uppsala ↗ .
