THE CONCEPTUAL INSPIRATION

The conceptual inspiration for our 14th congress

Within the realm of Family Therapy, there is room for creativity, improvisation, and play, as well as treatment manuals, effect sizes, and process research – all of which can make a difference. The possibility of making a difference that makes a difference – for our clients, for ourselves, and maybe for the world – might be called hope.

In challenging times, hope needs to be created over and over again. Preferably together with others, not excluding anyone.

We invite you to come and celebrate the broad variety of Family Therapy and Systemic Practice with the 40th Anniversary of the Nordic Family Therapy Congresses in Uppsala, August 17th - 20th 2027!

CALL FOR ABSTRACTS

Contribute to the congress

We want the 14th Nordic Congress to be a forum where everyone interested in family therapy, systemic practice and relational work can exchange ideas, thoughts, experiences, theories and research results. We wish to create a congress where research and creativity can blend, and where we can inspire each other to cocreate hope – for ourselves and for the families we meet.

We invite you to contribute with presentations inspired by the theme of the congress, and we encourage you to find expressions that best suit your contribution. It is up to you what format these can be: from clinical practice, research presentations, dissemination of art and culture, dialogues, posters, installations or verbal reflections.

SUBMISSION

Instructions

  • Abstracts must be submitted in English even if the presentation will be in a Scandinavian language.
  • The title of the abstract should not exceed 18 words.
  • The body of the abstract should not exceed 250 words excluding the title, author name(s) and affiliation details, 3-5 keywords and optional bibliography of maximum 5 references.
  • If abbreviations are used, the abbreviated term should be written out in full the first time it is used.
  • You will receive an automatic confirmation via email that your abstract was submitted successfully.
  • Authors wishing to include tables or figures can upload them separately as .DOC or PDF files.
  • Send in the abstract only once.
  • The abstracts for scientific presentation will be submitted to a peer review, coordinated by the Scientific Committee of the Nordic Family Therapy Congress.
  • All presenters of accepted abstracts are required to register, and to attend the congress at full price. Early bird is available until April 1, 2027.

CONTRIBUTION FORMATS

Proposal types

01

Scientific Oral Presentation

The oral paper presentation may be authored by an individual or a group. The oral presentations will be grouped in parallel sessions based on the sub-themes. More information about the abstract is provided below. A scientific oral presentation can be 20 or 40 minutes.

02

Scientific Poster Presentation

The poster presentation may be created by an individual or a group and will be displayed on a portable wall. At times, the author(s) of the poster will accompany it to answer people's questions. More information about the abstract is provided below.

03

Clinical Presentation & Workshops

A self-organised seminar of one or more presentations relevant to family therapy. This can be a presentation of clinical practices or interactive workshops. The amount of participation expected of participants should be specified in the abstract. These workshops may have an explicit agenda of training participants on a given topic or generating an interest group or a network for future collaboration. We especially welcome workshops involving creative family therapy practices. A workshop will be 45 minutes.

04

Dissemination of Art & Culture, Installations or Other Creative Presentations

The presentation has a free and creative form. The time frame will be 45 minutes.

ABSTRACT CONTENT

Guidelines

Empirical research projects

If your abstract is for a presentation based on one or more empirical research projects, it should include the following:

  • Background and purpose: description of the problem, study objectives, research question(s) and/or hypotheses.
  • Methods: study design, including a description of participants and selection strategies, data collection procedures, measures, and approaches to analysis.
  • Findings: specific results in summary form.
  • Conclusions and implications: description of the study’s main outcome(s) and impact on practice, policy or further research.

Practice-based presentations

If your abstract is for a presentation based on practice, it could include the following:

  • Background and purpose of the presentation.
  • A summary of the main points of the presentation.
  • Implications of your presentation for practice.
  • The expected amount of interaction between the presenter/presenters and participants.
